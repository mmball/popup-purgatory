# Playtest notes, 2026-10-16 (agent, bots and rendered frames)

Not a human playtest. Three sources: 43 rendered frames of bots playing through every
screen and state (a sped-up full workday to the clock-out screen, a crash on SP3, SP6 with
the full content pool, the Unlocks window, the full Start menu); 72 headless runs across
SP0/2/4/6 (greedy) and SP0/4 (heuristic driver) on the full pool, whole workday; and
reading the code paths the bots never exercise. Seeds and commands are given so anything
here can be reproduced. Nothing in M1 is answered by this: it cannot say whether the game
is fun.

## What works

- **The whole loop holds together.** A run starts, hours tick on the taskbar clock, breaks
  offer upgrades, 5 PM brings the clock-out dialog, the win screen types itself out with
  the new unlock and the next Service Pack, and the next run starts with the wider pool.
  No script errors in any session (`grep "SCRIPT ERROR"` on all logs: none).
- **The feel layer reads correctly in stills.** Pop-ins mid-fade, the fold-out sliver, the
  red flash on penalties, the darkened wallpaper at high RAM, the yellow "!" over imps that
  just chose a target, the "+7" and "Hour 4 done: +17 bytes" text, the static frame before
  the BSOD, the typed-out end screens. Nothing overlaps badly at 1280x720.
- **The Win98 chrome scales to the new content.** The Start menu with nine items and SP
  cost multipliers (25 B hut on SP6), the Unlocks window with four achievements and four
  perks, the clock-out dialog with relabelled buttons, all fit.
- **The targeted pop-ups do what they say.** Cookie banners are completed 100% by a policy
  that knows the recipe and punish the one that doesn't; chat widgets logged 233 dodges
  across 48 runs; leaks grew 63 times; the ladder stacks (SP2+ starts at 80 RAM, SP4+
  buildings cost more, SP6 grace is 2 s).
- **Determinism and the harness survived M2**: two juiced runs end in the same state as two
  unjuiced ones on the same seed.

## Bugs

Severity: P1 blocks or misleads a player; P2 visible wart; P3 internal.

1. **P3, fixed in this commit. Dev-arg values with hyphens were mangled.** The parser
   replaced `-` with `_` in the whole argument, so `--screenshot=/tmp/claude-0/x.png`
   wrote to a path that did not exist. Only the key is normalised now. Nothing shipped
   depends on dev args, but every batch and screenshot tool does.
2. **P2. Dismissing a Memory Leak floats "+0".** `_on_popup_closed` formats the reward
   unconditionally. Should read "leak stopped" (or nothing) when the reward is zero.
   Repro: `--unlock-all=1 --policy=random --seed=22`, watch any leak X.
3. **P2. "need N bytes" spam in placement mode.** Clicking a cell you cannot afford floats
   the message every click; the random bot produced a screen full of them (frames
   `crash_3` to `crash_5`). A person who misreads the cost will see the same. Fix: refuse
   to enter placement when the first copy is unaffordable (the Start menu already greys
   maxed items; grey unaffordable ones too), and float once per second at most.
4. **P2. Placement mode has no visible exit hint.** The grid appears and stays until a
   click lands or Esc/right-click, neither of which is on screen. Add "Esc cancels" to the
   ghost label or the taskbar.
5. **P2. "Remind me later" frees the window without the fold animation**, unlike every
   other close. Cosmetic inconsistency; route it through `Juice.pop_out`.
6. **P2. Hour tally and HOUR banner can overlap** for about half a second when both are on
   screen (frame `day_11`). Offset the tally higher or delay the banner by 0.4 s.
7. **P3. Windowed runs under the dummy audio driver report leaked playbacks at exit.**
   Harmless (engine-side, only when there is a window but no real audio device), but it
   puts `ERROR:` lines in CI render logs. Headless runs are already silent.
8. **P3 (probable, not reproduced). A click on a folding window's buttons during the 0.14 s
   fold still reaches them.** `is_closing` guards double rewards, but a trap button on a
   folding ad could still spawn. Set `mouse_filter` to IGNORE on the children too, or
   reparent the folding window into a non-interactive layer.

## Improvements

- **The cookie banner needs one line of teaching.** Its punishment (X spawns two more) is
  invisible until it has happened, and a player who keeps X-ing spirals 1, 2, 4, 8. The
  "consent fatigue" float is too late and too small. Options: the first banner of a run
  carries a title "(the X makes more)", or the first X on a cookie opens a one-time hint.
  This belongs to M3 onboarding but it is the single most likely rage-quit in the game.
- **Hour 4 is a cliff with the full pool.** Greedy deaths cluster at hours 4 and 5 on every
  tier (SP0: 1 at hour 2, 2 at hour 4, 1 at hour 5; SP2: 3 and 3 at hours 4 and 5). That is
  where chat (hour 3) and leak (hour 4) join cookies (hour 2). Consider staggering the
  unlockable types across hours 2, 4 and 6, or weighting them lower in their first hour.
- **The heuristic at one decision per second, a fair proxy for a competent human, clocks
  out in 4 of 8 SP0 runs** on the full pool and dies in hours 5 to 6 otherwise. A 50% win
  rate for a decent player on the base tier may be right for a roguelite; it is probably
  too hard for a first-time player, who also plays worse than the heuristic. The base
  pool (no unlocks) is easier: greedy never died there. So the game gets harder as you
  unlock things, which is the opposite of the sideways-unlock promise. The counters
  (Consent Manager, Help Desk, Garbage Collector) exist but are only offered sometimes.
  Candidate fix: make the counter building or upgrade guaranteed to appear within two
  hours of its pop-up's first spawn.
- **Imps lose value late.** Untrained imps cannot touch cookies, chats dodge them, and
  leaks pay 0 to dismiss. Imp share of closes falls to about 19% on the unlockable types
  versus 35% on base types. That makes the dojo mandatory rather than a choice. Either
  let untrained imps reject cookies slowly (no spawn, long work time) or make the Help
  Desk cheaper and earlier.
- **Rising RAM tone may grate.** It repeats every 0.35 to 1.2 s above 75% RAM. Needs an
  ear; a person should judge it before anyone else hears it. Cap it at one per 2 s or make
  it a single sustained tone that rises.
- **The random bot sometimes sits in placement mode for a whole hour** (frames `crash_13`
  to `crash_16`), which is a bot problem, not a game one, but it shows the grid is a
  modal state with no timeout. Fine for humans with the exit hint above.
- **Garbage Collector correlates with worse runs** (mean score 527 with versus 677
  without). That is selection, not causation: it is offered in leak-heavy runs. Keep it,
  but it suggests the leak itself is the problem when it appears early.
- **Captcha, survey, newsletter and toolbar have 1 to 5% bot completion.** That is the
  bots' limitation (no recipes), not the design's; it means the balance tables undercount
  what a human earns from them. A human-speed completion recipe for each is M4 harness work.

## Balance observations (full pool, whole workday, 12 greedy runs per tier)

| tier | clock-outs | deaths | mean score | mean hour reached |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| SP0 | 8 | 4 | 708 | 6.6 |
| SP2 | 6 | 6 | 665 | 6.2 |
| SP4 | 2 | 10 | 226 | 3.8 |
| SP6 | 2 | 10 | 380 | 4.8 |

SP4 (buildings +25%) hits greedy harder than SP6 (grace 2 s), which says the ladder's
steps are not monotonic in difficulty for this policy; SP5 (slower imps) and SP6 stack on
SP4 anyway, so a player feels them in order, but SP4's jump is the big one. Candidate:
move the cost step to SP5 and the imp-speed step to SP4, or soften it to +15%.

Heuristic driver, 8 runs each: SP0 4 clock-outs, 4 deaths (hours 5 to 6); SP4 5
clock-outs, 3 deaths. The heuristic handles SP4 better than greedy because it builds
before it spends on anything else.

Pop-up table (all 72 runs): ads and errors are 43% of spawns; cookies average 5.7 s on
screen against 2 to 3 s for everything else; 45 error multiplications; 64 ad traps and 51
accept-alls, all bot-inflicted.

## Suggested order of fixes

1. Bugs 2, 3, 4, 5, 6 (an afternoon; all cosmetic or UX, no balance change).
2. Stagger the unlockable pop-ups' first hours and guarantee their counters: a sweep with
   `--tune=popup.chat.min_wave=4,popup.leak.min_wave=6` on the same seeds will show whether
   the hour-4 cliff moves.
3. Bug 8 probe: a bot that clicks the trap button during folds; fix if it reproduces.
4. Then M1 for real: the numbers above are bot numbers.

## Reproduce

```sh
G=/path/to/godot
# sped-up workday to the win screen, 22 frames
tools/screenshot.sh is not needed; use the dev flags directly:
xvfb-run -a -s "-screen 0 1280x720x24" $G --path . --rendering-driver opengl3 --audio-driver Dummy --fixed-fps 60 -- \
  --screen=desktop --autoplay=1 --seed=21 --unlock-all=1 --runs=2 --bsod-linger=12 \
  --tune=run.wave_base=6,run.wave_step=0 --fresh-meta --no-telemetry --noboot --clock="12:00 AM" \
  --screenshot=out/day.png --delay=4 --shots=22
# per-tier batches
for t in 0 2 4 6; do BOT_EXTRA="--tier=$t --unlock-all=1" tools/botrun.sh $G 12 greedy $((100+t)) 9; done
python3 tools/policy_driver.py $G --backend heuristic --runs 8 --seed 300 --max-wave 9 --unlock-all
python3 tools/balance.py runs/*.jsonl --events runs/telemetry
```
